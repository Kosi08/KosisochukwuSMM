PlushUGC

APPLICATION EXERCISE — NOT CLIENT WORK

This section demonstrates how I think based on the role requirements and publicly observable content. It does not imply that I have worked with PlushUGC.

01 / Start with the problem, not the product

For UGC and growth-focused content, I would look for the problem a viewer immediately recognises before introducing the product. The hook should make the audience feel seen before asking them to care about the brand.

EXAMPLE: “As a dark-skinned girl, I find it had to get skincare products that won't alter my skin tone…”

02 / Give quiet UGC a story

When a UGC video has little or no spoken dialogue, the story still has to be communicated through proper product placement, on-screen text, sound design, pacing, visual progression and results-based moments.

03 / Research → deconstruct → adapt

I would break down successful content into its mechanism — hook, pain point, promise, proof, pacing, visual contrast and CTA. Then I would shape the content around the brand's audience and offer rather than copying the surface idea.

HOOK LAB

A hook should capture the first three seconds.

PAIN POINT

A fashion designer looking to attract higher paying clients.

“Is it not just chalk and thread?...”

Clients exclaim at the price and underrate the worth of the service.

CONTRAST

“You keep hearing this because you haven't made your brand look worth the price.”

This describes the pain point and acts as a rehook, drawing the audience to learn the solution.

CURIOSITY

“Start posting only high quality content.....”

This describes a solution and keeps the audience engaged.

EDITOR / DESIGNER BRIEF

How I would communicate creative direction

HOOK Make the first frame understandable even without context.
PACING Remove dead space and make every visual change earn its place.
TYPE Keep typography readable and subordinate to the message.
PROOF Show the result, transformation, reaction or evidence early.
SOUND Use voice, music and effects deliberately.
CTA Give the viewer one clear next action.
